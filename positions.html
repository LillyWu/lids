Thank you for your interest in joining LIDS at the University of Alberta! We are currently recruiting PhD and MSc students and one postdoctoral researcher. If you are interested in joining our group, please first fill out our interest form and read the following instructions on how to apply to the related graduate programs at UAlberta.

Prospective Postdoctoral Researchers

We are actively recruiting one postdoctoral researcher. Applicants should have a strong publication record in leading systems or machine learning venues, or substantial software engineering experience developing distributed systems or machine learning systems. If you are interested, please fill out the interest form and email us with a brief introduction.

Prospective Graduate Students

We primarily recruit PhD and MSc students through the Department of Computing Science at the University of Alberta. Please review the application and admissions guidance and submit your official application through the graduate application portal.

  • Select Li Wu as one of your potential supervisors in your application, if that option is available.
  • Fill out the interest form so that we can learn about your background and research interests.

There is no need to email separately to inquire about your application. Please note that the LIDS interest form does not replace the official University of Alberta graduate application.

Current and Incoming Graduate Students

If you are already at the University of Alberta or are about to begin your studies, please email us with your CV and a brief note about your background and research interests.
